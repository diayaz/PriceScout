Store guide

Each store is cheapest for different things. Here is what every store is good at and what to watch out for before you buy.

The four stores we compare

Amazon

Best for: electronics

Huge catalog and fast delivery. Often the cheapest for smartwatches and denim.

  • Easy returns
  • Watch out: shipping can raise the final price

Pinduoduo

Best for: basics & clothing

Group-buy discounts make everyday items very cheap, like hoodies and earbuds.

  • Lowest prices on basics
  • Watch out: longer delivery, quality varies

Poizon

Best for: sneakers

Every pair goes through an authenticity check before it is shipped to you.

  • Rare and limited releases
  • Watch out: prices on hyped pairs change fast

Official Brand Site

Best for: warranty

Buying straight from the brand means the item is 100% original.

  • Full warranty and official returns
  • Watch out: usually the highest price

3 tips before you buy

Compare the same model

Check the exact model name and size. A "similar" item is not the same deal.

Count the shipping

A low price plus expensive delivery can cost more than the official site.

Wait for a drop

Not in a hurry? Set a price alert and we will email you when it gets cheaper.
